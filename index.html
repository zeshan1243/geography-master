How well do you know the world?

There are 195 sovereign countries on this site — 193 United Nations member states plus Vatican City and Palestine. Between them they cover six inhabited continents, 195 capital cities and every flag flown at the UN.

Most people can name perhaps thirty or forty countries from memory. Getting from there to all 195 is a matter of repetition, and repetition is easier when it looks like a game. Each round here is ten questions, takes about a minute, and shows you the right answer the moment you get one wrong — that immediate correction is what makes the facts stick.

Start with the flag quiz on easy, move to capitals when flags feel comfortable, and use the mixed quiz to check what has actually stuck.
