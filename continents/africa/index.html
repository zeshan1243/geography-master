🌍 Africa

Africa is the second largest and second most populous continent. It holds 54 sovereign countries, the Sahara — the largest hot desert on Earth — and the Nile, one of the world's longest rivers.

Africa facts

  • Africa is the only continent that spans both the northern and southern temperate zones.
  • Nigeria is the most populous African country, with over 220 million people.
  • The Sahara covers roughly a third of the continent's land area.
  • More than 1,000 languages are spoken across Africa.
