🌎 South America

South America is home to the Amazon rainforest, the Andes — the longest mountain range in the world — and just 12 sovereign countries, making it a favourite starting point for geography learners.

South America facts

  • The Amazon carries more water than any other river on Earth.
  • The Andes run for about 7,000 kilometres along the western edge of the continent.
  • Bolivia and Paraguay are the continent's only landlocked countries.
  • Angel Falls in Venezuela is the world's tallest uninterrupted waterfall.
