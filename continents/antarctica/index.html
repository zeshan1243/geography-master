🐧 Antarctica

Antarctica is the southernmost continent and the only one with no permanent population and no sovereign countries. It is governed by the Antarctic Treaty and holds about 70% of the world's fresh water as ice.

Antarctica facts

  • The lowest natural temperature ever recorded, −89.2 °C, was measured at Vostok Station.
  • Antarctica is technically the largest desert in the world.
  • No country owns Antarctica; the Antarctic Treaty reserves it for scientific research.
  • Its ice sheet averages about 1.9 kilometres thick.
