🌏 Oceania

Oceania spans Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea and thousands of Pacific islands. It is the smallest continent by land area and by population.

Oceania facts

  • Australia accounts for the vast majority of Oceania's land area.
  • Nauru is the world's smallest island country at just 21 km².
  • The Great Barrier Reef is the largest living structure on the planet.
  • Many Oceanian states use the Australian or US dollar rather than their own currency.
