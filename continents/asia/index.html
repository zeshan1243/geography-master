🌏 Asia

Asia is the largest continent by both area and population. It contains the world's highest mountain, its most populous countries, and some of the oldest continuously inhabited cities.

Asia facts

  • Around 60% of all people on Earth live in Asia.
  • China and India each have more than 1.4 billion residents.
  • The Dead Sea shore is the lowest land point on the planet.
  • Asia contains both the highest and lowest natural elevations on land.
