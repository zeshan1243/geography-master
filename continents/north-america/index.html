🌎 North America

North America stretches from the Arctic tundra of Canada and Greenland to the tropical islands of the Caribbean, and includes 23 sovereign countries.

North America facts

  • Canada has the longest coastline of any country on Earth.
  • Mexico City is one of the largest metropolitan areas in the world.
  • The Caribbean holds 13 of the continent's 23 sovereign states.
  • The Great Lakes contain about a fifth of the world's surface fresh water.
