Chad

N'Djamena · Central Africa

Chad is a country in Africa. Its capital city is N'Djamena, the official currency is the Central African CFA Franc, and the main language is French.

With a population of roughly 18.3 million people spread over 1,284,000 km², Chad is the 66th most populous country in the world and the 20th largest by area. That works out at about 14 people per square kilometre.

Chad shares land borders with Libya, Sudan, Central African Republic, Cameroon, Nigeria and Niger — 6 neighbours in all. Its largest urban centres are N'Djamena, Moundou and Sarh. The highest point in the country is Emi Koussi, at 3,445 metres.

For scale, it covers almost exactly the same area as Peru, while its population is closest to that of Ecuador.

Chad: things worth knowing

  • Lake Chad has shrunk by roughly 90% since the 1960s through drought and irrigation.
  • Emi Koussi is a dormant volcano in the Tibesti range, the highest point in the Sahara.
  • Chad is landlocked, and its capital lies over 1,000 kilometres from the nearest port.
