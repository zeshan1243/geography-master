Vietnam

Hanoi · Southeast Asia

Vietnam is a country in Asia. Its capital city is Hanoi, the official currency is the Vietnamese Dong, and the main language is Vietnamese.

With a population of roughly 98.9 million people spread over 331,212 km², Vietnam is the 16th most populous country in the world and the 66th largest by area. That works out at about 299 people per square kilometre.

Vietnam shares land borders with China, Laos and Cambodia — 3 neighbours in all. Its largest urban centres are Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi and Haiphong. The highest point in the country is Fansipan, at 3,147 metres.

For scale, it covers almost exactly the same area as Malaysia, while its population is closest to that of DR Congo.

Vietnam: things worth knowing

  • Vietnam is the world's second largest coffee exporter, specialising in robusta.
  • Hạ Long Bay contains roughly 1,600 limestone islands and islets, most of them uninhabited.
  • The country stretches over 1,600 kilometres north to south but narrows to under 50 kilometres at its waist.
