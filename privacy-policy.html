Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Geography Master mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Geography Master is a fully offline learning game. We do not require an account, and we do not collect or sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location. The App works offline and has no login or user accounts.

Data stored on your device only: Your quiz scores, accuracy, streaks, achievements, and learning progress are saved locally on your device using on-device storage (AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or use the in-app "Reset Progress" option.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Device information (device model, operating system, language, coarse region)
  • App interaction and ad-performance data (e.g. ad views and clicks)
  • Approximate, non-precise location derived from the above

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. We do not control the data practices of Google. Please review Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. Your Choices & Controls

  • Reset advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking / Apple Advertising).
  • Limit ad personalization: Your device settings let you opt out of personalized ads.
  • Consent (EEA/UK): Where required, the App presents a consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads.
  • Clear your data: Use "Reset Progress" in the App, or uninstall the App, to remove all locally stored data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience educational game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the App is directed to or used by children in your region, applicable ad settings (such as tagging requests for child-directed treatment) are used to help limit data collection. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

5. Data Security & Retention

Because progress data is stored only on your device, its security depends on your device. We retain no personal data on our servers (we operate none). Advertising data is retained by Google according to Google's own policies.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service, which may process data as described above:

  • Google AdMob — Privacy Policy

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Us
