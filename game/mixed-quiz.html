    About the mixed geography quiz

    The mixed quiz pulls from flags, capitals, countries, continents, landmarks and oceans in a single round. It is the closest thing here to an honest general test, and it is the fastest way to find out which category is letting you down.

    Because questions rotate between categories, you cannot settle into one kind of thinking. That makes it noticeably harder than any single game at the same difficulty, even though the individual questions are drawn from the same pools.

    This is also the game behind the daily challenge, where every player gets the same ten questions, generated from the date so they change at midnight.

    Difficulty and scoring

    Every round is ten questions across four levels — easy covers the countries most people can already name, expert reaches the microstates and the genuinely obscure. A correct answer is worth 100 points, with a bonus for answering inside five seconds and further bonuses for streaks. A wrong answer scores nothing and resets the streak, but never ends the round. The full scoring breakdown is on the about page.
