    About the country shape quiz

    Ask someone to picture Italy and they will see the boot, not the flag or the capital. Outlines are how most people actually store a map, and this is the only game here that tests that directly.

    Some silhouettes are unmistakable: Italy, Chile, Norway, India, Japan. Others are far harder than you would expect, because a country's shape is the part of it we rehearse least. Landlocked states in central Africa and central Asia give almost nothing away.

    Outlines are drawn at world-map scale and then enlarged, so tiny states are excluded — 163 of the 195 countries have an outline distinct enough to be worth asking about.

    Difficulty and scoring

    Every round is ten questions across four levels — easy covers the countries most people can already name, expert reaches the microstates and the genuinely obscure. A correct answer is worth 100 points, with a bonus for answering inside five seconds and further bonuses for streaks. A wrong answer scores nothing and resets the streak, but never ends the round. The full scoring breakdown is on the about page.
