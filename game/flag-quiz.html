    About the flag quiz

    Flags are the fastest route into world geography because they are visual. Most people can recognise a flag long before they can recall the matching capital, and that recognition then acts as a hook for everything else about the country.

    The hard rounds lean on genuine near-duplicates. Chad and Romania are almost indistinguishable. Monaco and Indonesia differ only in proportion. Ireland and Ivory Coast are mirror images. Australia and New Zealand both carry the Union Jack and the Southern Cross, differing in the number and colour of the stars.

    Flags render using your device's own emoji font, so the exact drawing style depends on your operating system. The colours and layout are the same everywhere.

    Difficulty and scoring

    Every round is ten questions across four levels — easy covers the countries most people can already name, expert reaches the microstates and the genuinely obscure. A correct answer is worth 100 points, with a bonus for answering inside five seconds and further bonuses for streaks. A wrong answer scores nothing and resets the streak, but never ends the round. The full scoring breakdown is on the about page.
